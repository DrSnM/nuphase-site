I spend time understanding how your business runs, then write you a plan: where AI will save time or lift the quality of what you produce, where it won't, and what to try first. Fixed price, based in Adelaide, working remotely across Australia.

How it works

  1. Talk through how the work gets done. I ask what you do, what information you hold and what you send out. I look at real examples of the inputs and outputs.
  2. Work out where AI helps and where it doesn't. Each task gets looked at for time saved, quality, cost and what could go wrong, including what happens to your data.
  3. Get a written plan. A prioritised plan built around your business, with one pilot described in enough detail to try, and a way to tell whether it worked.

Why me

My career has been about taking slow, manual, expert-dependent work and making it something a team can rely on. AI is the newest tool for that job, and I’ll help you point it at the work that will repay it.

  • I’ve sat in your chair. I ran Ziltek as CEO: five full-time staff plus interns, $1.3M to $1.8M a year in turnover, and every hour and dollar counted. Your plan will fit what a small team can afford to run, not what a big company could.
  • I’ve seen what good automation does. At Ziltek we took a manual, judgement-heavy calibration process and rebuilt it as a pipeline more than 90% automated. Turnaround fell from 2 days to under 1 hour, and the team’s time went to work that needed them. That’s the kind of change I look for in your business.
  • I build with these tools myself. Groundwork is a live product I built with AI coding tools, so I know where they save weeks and where they need a careful eye. Here’s how I scope, build and review the work.
  • I’ll tell you when the answer is no. I’m a physicist with a PhD, trained to test an idea before trusting it. A plan worth paying for includes the places not to use AI, so your money goes where it pays back.
