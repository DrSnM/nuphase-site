How it works

  1. Talk through how the work gets done. I ask what you do, what information you hold and what you send out. I look at real examples of the inputs and outputs.
  2. Work out where AI helps and where it doesn't. Each task gets looked at for time saved, quality, cost and what could go wrong, including what happens to your data.
  3. Get a written plan. A prioritised plan built around your business, with one pilot described in enough detail to try, and a way to tell whether it worked.
